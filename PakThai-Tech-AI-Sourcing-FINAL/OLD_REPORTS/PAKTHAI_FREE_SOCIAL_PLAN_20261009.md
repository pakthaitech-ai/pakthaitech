## What is free
- Facebook Page: organic posts, product photos, short videos, and Reels.
- Instagram: organic Reels, Stories, and posts.
- YouTube: Shorts made from the same vertical product video.
- Relevant Pakistan computer-market/community groups: post only where business promotion is allowed; follow each group's rules and avoid repeated copy-paste spam.
- Google Business Profile: free for an eligible real business that can complete Google's verification. Do not create a fake address or a listing for a business that does not meet eligibility rules.
- Paid boosts, Meta Ads, and YouTube paid campaigns are not free. No ad budget or billing should be enabled for this plan.

## Seven-day posting schedule (reuse one video across Facebook, Instagram, and YouTube Shorts)
1. Day 1 — Brand intro: “PAKTHAI TECH — IT hardware and computer components for customers and retailers across Pakistan.” CTA: browse the catalogue.
2. Day 2 — Product category: SSDs and storage. Show product images from the live store; do not invent stock or prices.
3. Day 3 — PC build basics: explain how to choose a compatible PSU, RAM, or SSD. CTA: message the exact model and required quantity for a quote.
4. Day 4 — Retailer/dealer invitation: invite computer shops and resellers to send their required items, city, quantity, and target dealer price.
5. Day 5 — How to request a quote: screen-record the store, open a product, and show how to contact PAKTHAI TECH. Do not show customer details or private dashboard information.
6. Day 6 — FAQ: explain that availability, shipping, and final landed cost are confirmed before an order; do not promise delivery dates without verification.
7. Day 7 — Weekly catalogue reminder: feature 3–5 real product listings from the live store and link to the catalogue.

## Posting rules
- Use only product photos and prices that are currently shown on the live store.
- Add the store link to every post where the platform permits it.
- Reply to genuine comments promptly; move quote discussions to the business email or the store's working contact channel.
- Do not buy followers, scrape group members, mass-DM users, or post repeatedly where promotion is prohibited.
- Track post date, platform, reach, clicks, enquiries, and confirmed orders in a simple spreadsheet.
