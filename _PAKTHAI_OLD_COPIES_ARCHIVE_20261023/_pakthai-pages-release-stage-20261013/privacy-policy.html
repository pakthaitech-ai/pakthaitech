Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 12, 2026

PakThai Tech (“we,” “us,” or “our”) operates the PakThai Tech online storefront and customer-support channels. This policy explains how we handle information you choose to provide when browsing the store or contacting us.

We keep information only for as long as reasonably needed for the purposes above, including handling requests, maintaining business records, resolving disputes, and meeting legal requirements. We use reasonable safeguards, but no online service can guarantee absolute security.

Your choices and requests

You may ask to access, correct, or delete personal information associated with your inquiry, subject to legal and legitimate record-keeping requirements. See our data deletion instructions. You can contact us using WhatsApp support or the live-chat option on our store.

Children

Our store is not intended to collect personal information from children knowingly. If you believe a child has submitted personal information, contact us so we can review the request.

Changes

We may update this policy as our services change. The effective date above indicates the latest version.
